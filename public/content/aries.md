# Aries ♈

## Class: Barbarian

**The Aries Fury**

## Dates

## Element

🔥 Fire

## Ruling Planet

## Description

The Barbarian channels the fiery, impulsive energy of Aries, representing raw courage, aggression, and leadership. Rage is flavored as a connection to the warrior spirit of Aries, granting unstoppable aggression in battle. They tap into fire-based abilities, representing Aries' connection to the element of fire, and can summon animal spirits like the ram or divine manifestations of an Aries warrior.

Power through fury, instinct, and raw emotion. Rage is the soul igniting with primordial starfire. Seen as shock troops, champions, or living armoury. Strength through action, not restraint.

## Character Creation

### Hit Dice

1d12 per barbarian level

### Primary Ability

Strength

### Saving Throw Proficiencies

Strength, Constitution

### Armor Proficiencies

Light armor, medium armor, shields

### Armoury Proficiencies

Simple armoury, martial armoury

### Skill Choices

**Choose 2 from:** Animal Handling, Athletics, Intimidation, Nature, Perception, Survival

### Starting Equipment

**Choose (a) or (b) for each:**

- (a) A greataxe OR (b) Any martial melee armoury
- (a) Two handaxes OR (b) Any simple armoury
- An explorer's pack and four javelins

### Key Features

- **Rage:** Enter a battle fury, gaining damage bonus and resistance to physical damage (2/day at level 1)
- **Unarmored Defense:** AC = 10 + DEX modifier + CON modifier when not wearing armor
- **Reckless Attack:** Gain advantage on melee attacks but enemies have advantage against you
- **Danger Sense:** Advantage on DEX saves against visible effects

### Primal Paths (Subclass at Level 3)

**Choose one:**

- **Path of the Berserker:** Frenzy for extra attacks, intimidating presence
- **Path of the Totem Warrior:** Spirit animals grant special powers (Bear, Eagle, Wolf)
- **Path of the Ancestral Guardian:** Ancestors protect and avenge
- **Path of the Storm Herald:** Aura of elemental damage
- **Path of the Zealot:** Divine fury and warrior from beyond death
- **Path of the Beast:** Transform with natural armoury
- **Path of Wild Magic:** Chaotic magical effects during rage

### 🔥 Elemental Affinity: Fire

**Recommended Path:** Path of the Storm Herald (Desert aura) - Channel fire damage in an aura around you, embodying Aries' fiery rage.

**Alternative Paths:**

- Path of the Zealot - Divine fire and fury
- Path of the Berserker - Pure burning rage

**Elemental Flavor:** Describe your rage as flames erupting from within, your attacks leaving scorch marks, your battle cry like a roaring inferno.

## Traits

**Recommended Ability Scores:** STR (highest), CON (high), DEX (moderate)

**Suggested Equipment Packages:**

- **Fire Warrior:** Greataxe, javelins, medium armor
- **Ram's Charge:** Warhammer, shield, handaxes
- **Primal Fury:** Greatsword, explorer's pack, tribal totems

## Strengths

High damage output, exceptional durability, powerful in melee combat, resistant to physical damage during rage

## Weaknesses

Vulnerable to magic, limited ranged options, rage is a limited resource, low AC without armor

## Compatibility

Works well with tactical support classes. Pairs excellently with healers and ranged damage dealers who can stay behind frontline
