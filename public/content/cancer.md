## Description

The Druid takes on the role of the nurturer and protector, using the power of nature to safeguard others, much like Cancer's emotional depth and loyalty. They summon creatures with deep connections to the ocean or shells, like giant crabs or sea creatures. Their powers are centered around emotional connection, protection of loved ones, and deep intuitive magic.

Power through emotional bonds and nature. Magic is protective, adaptive, and deeply personal. Shapeshifting reflects instinct and loyalty. Nature is family, not a resource.
