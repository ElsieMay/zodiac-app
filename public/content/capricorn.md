# Capricorn ♑

## Class: Fighter

**The Capricorn Strategist**

## Dates


## Element

🪨 Earth

## Ruling Planet


## Description

The Fighter becomes a methodical, disciplined warrior, embodying Capricorn's ambition, patience, and tactical planning. Their Battlemaster subclass reflects Capricorn's pragmatism, with tactical maneuvers that rely on preparation and long-term planning. They emphasize longevity and the ability to fight for what's earned, always training, improving, and preparing for battles ahead.

Power through discipline, endurance, and mastery. Martial skill is honed through relentless effort. Often generals, knights, mercenaries. Victory is earned, never gifted.

## Character Creation

### Hit Dice
1d10 per fighter level

### Primary Ability
Strength or Dexterity

### Saving Throw Proficiencies
Strength, Constitution

### Armor Proficiencies
All armor, shields

### Weapon Proficiencies
Simple weapons, martial weapons

### Skill Choices
**Choose 2 from:** Acrobatics, Animal Handling, Athletics, History, Insight, Intimidation, Perception, Survival

### Starting Equipment
**Choose (a) or (b) for each:**
- (a) Chain mail OR (b) Leather armor, longbow, and 20 arrows
- (a) A martial weapon and a shield OR (b) Two martial weapons
- (a) A light crossbow and 20 bolts OR (b) Two handaxes
- (a) A dungeoneer's pack OR (b) An explorer's pack

### Key Features
- **Fighting Style:** Choose at level 1 (Archery, Defense, Dueling, Great Weapon Fighting, Protection, Two-Weapon Fighting)
- **Second Wind:** Heal yourself as bonus action (1d10 + fighter level), recharges on short rest
- **Action Surge:** Take an additional action on your turn (level 2, recharges on short rest)
- **Extra Attack:** Attack twice per action (level 5), increases to 3 at level 11, 4 at level 20
- **Indomitable:** Reroll a failed save (level 9, increases with level)

### Martial Archetypes (Subclass at Level 3)
**Choose one:**
- **Battle Master:** Combat maneuvers, tactical superiority dice
- **Champion:** Improved critical hits, remarkable athlete, bonus fighting style
- **Eldritch Knight:** Learn wizard spells, weapon bond, war magic
- **Arcane Archer:** Magic arrows with various effects
- **Cavalier:** Mounted combat expert, protect allies, mark enemies
- **Samurai:** Fighting spirit, advantage on attacks, elegant courtier
- **Echo Knight:** Create echo of yourself, attack and swap places
- **Psi Warrior:** Psionic powers for damage, defense, and mobility
- **Rune Knight:** Inscribe runes for various effects, grow to giant size
- **Purple Dragon Knight:** Inspire and heal allies

### 🪨 Elemental Affinity: Earth
**Recommended Archetype:** Rune Knight - Inscribe ancient earth runes, grow to giant size, embody the strength and endurance of mountains.

**Alternative Archetypes:**
- Battle Master - Tactical, grounded combat mastery
- Champion - Steady, reliable, enduring warrior
- Samurai - Disciplined earth philosophy

**Elemental Flavor:** Your attacks feel heavy like landslides, your defense is unyielding as bedrock, your strategy is patient as erosion.

**Recommended Runes (if Rune Knight):**
- Stone Rune (endurance and insight)
- Hill Rune (resistance)
- Storm Rune (solid foundation with tactical awareness)

### Fighting Styles
- **Archery:** +2 bonus to ranged attack rolls
- **Defense:** +1 AC while wearing armor
- **Dueling:** +2 damage when wielding one weapon and no other weapons
- **Great Weapon Fighting:** Reroll 1s and 2s on damage dice with two-handed weapons
- **Protection:** Impose disadvantage on attacks against nearby allies
- **Two-Weapon Fighting:** Add ability modifier to damage of off-hand attacks
- **Blind Fighting:** Blindsight 10 feet
- **Interception:** Reduce damage to nearby ally

### Battle Master Maneuvers (Examples)
- **Riposte:** Counterattack when enemy misses
- **Trip Attack:** Knock enemy prone
- **Disarming Attack:** Force enemy to drop weapon
- **Goading Attack:** Taunt enemy to attack you

## Traits

**Recommended Ability Scores:** STR or DEX (highest), CON (high), other abilities moderate

**Suggested Equipment Packages:**
- **Disciplined Strategist:** Longsword + shield, chain mail, Defense, Battle Master
- **Methodical Duelist:** Rapier (DEX build), leather armor, Dueling, high DEX
- **Patient Tactician:** Greatsword, chain mail, Great Weapon Fighting, Champion
- **Calculated Archer:** Longbow, leather armor, Archery, Arcane Archer

**Suggested Skill Selections:**
- Strategic Leader: History, Insight
- Disciplined Warrior: Athletics, Perception
- Tactical Commander: Intimidation, Perception

## Strengths

Most attacks per turn, proficient with all weapons and armor, Action Surge is incredibly powerful, high HP, multiple fighting styles, extremely versatile

## Weaknesses

No magical abilities (except Eldritch Knight), limited out-of-combat utility, fewer skill proficiencies, straightforward playstyle can lack variety

## Compatibility

Works in any party composition. Can fill tank, damage dealer, or support role. Self-sufficient with Second Wind. Pairs well with any support or control casters
