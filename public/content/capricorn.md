## Description

The Fighter becomes a methodical, disciplined warrior, embodying Capricorn's ambition, patience, and tactical planning. Their Battlemaster subclass reflects Capricorn's pragmatism, with tactical maneuvers that rely on preparation and long-term planning. They emphasize longevity and the ability to fight for what's earned, always training, improving, and preparing for battles ahead.

Power through discipline, endurance, and mastery. Martial skill is honed through relentless effort. Often generals, knights, mercenaries. Victory is earned, never gifted.
