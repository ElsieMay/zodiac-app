# Caucasian Eagle 🦅

## Class: Ranger

**The Eternal Hunter**

## Origin

Greek Mythology

## Element

⚡ Storm

## Domain

Sky and Retribution

## Description

The Caucasian Eagle Ranger embodies relentless pursuit and divine punishment. Named for Zeus's eagle that tormented Prometheus, these warriors are hunters who never abandon their prey. They command the skies, wielding storm-infused arrows and summoning aerial strikes. Their tracking abilities are supernatural—once they mark a target, escape becomes impossible.

Power through persistence, precision, and aerial dominance. They are the wrath of the heavens made manifest, patient hunters who strike from above with thunderous force.

## Traits

- Sky Hunter: Enhanced tracking and aerial reconnaissance
- Storm Arrow: Lightning-infused ranged attacks
- Eagle's Dive: Devastating aerial strike ability

## Strengths

- Superior ranged combat capability
- Exceptional tracking and reconnaissance
- Mobility through flight or enhanced movement

## Weaknesses

- Less effective in enclosed spaces
- Vulnerable to anti-air abilities
- Requires distance to maximize effectiveness

## Compatibility

Synergizes with earth-bound warriors who can flush out targets. Struggles against heavily armored foes who can weather ranged assaults.
