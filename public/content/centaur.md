# Centaur 🏹

## Class: Cavalry Archer

**The Swift Warrior**

## Origin

Greek Mythology

## Element

🌿 Nature

## Domain

Wilderness and Wisdom

## Description

The Centaur Cavalry Archer merges human intellect with bestial power, embodying both the scholar and the warrior. These half-human, half-horse beings possess unmatched mobility and versatility in combat. They excel at hit-and-run tactics, combining archery with devastating charges, while their deep connection to nature grants them wisdom and healing abilities.

Power through mobility, versatility, and natural harmony. They are philosophers who fight, healers who hunt, bridging civilization and wilderness with every gallop.

## Traits

- Unstoppable Charge: Devastating mounted melee attacks
- Rapid Fire: Multiple arrows while moving at full speed
- Nature's Bond: Healing and nature magic abilities

## Strengths

- Exceptional mobility and positioning
- Versatile combat style (ranged and melee)
- Natural healing and support capabilities

## Weaknesses

- Large target, easier to hit
- Struggles in tight spaces or rough terrain
- Divided focus between warrior and sage roles

## Compatibility

Ideal for mobile warfare and skirmishing. Works well with defensive units who can anchor flanks. Less effective in siege situations.
