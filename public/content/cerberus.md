# Cerberus 🐺

## Class: Guardian

**The Three-Faced Sentinel**

## Origin

Greek Mythology

## Element

🔥 Infernal Fire

## Domain

Underworld and Protection

## Description

The Cerberus Guardian is the ultimate protector, modeled after the three-headed hound that guards the gates of Hades. These warriors possess supernatural awareness, able to watch multiple directions simultaneously. They excel at defensive combat, area denial, and protecting allies. Their abilities channel hellfire and primal fury, making them nearly impossible to bypass.

Power through vigilance, endurance, and territorial dominance. They are immovable objects, sentinels who hold the line against any threat, living fortresses that devour those foolish enough to advance.

## Traits

- Three-Fold Awareness: Cannot be flanked or surprised
- Hellfire Aura: Damages nearby enemies passively
- Guardian's Fury: Increased power when protecting allies

## Strengths

- Exceptional defensive capabilities
- Area control and denial
- High durability and threat generation

## Weaknesses

- Limited mobility
- Vulnerable to ranged kiting
- Struggles when forced to pursue

## Compatibility

Essential for defensive formations and protecting vulnerable allies. Synergizes with ranged damage dealers. Less useful in highly mobile engagements.
