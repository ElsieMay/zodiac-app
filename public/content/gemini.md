# Gemini ♊

## Class: Wizard

**The Gemini Scholar**

## Dates


## Element

🌀 Air

## Ruling Planet


## Description

The Wizard channels the curiosity, adaptability, and communication skills of Gemini, specializing in mental magic and diversity. They focus on illusion magic, mind manipulation, and divination, reflecting Gemini's dual nature and adaptability. Their spells manipulate, outwit, or predict outcomes based on the duality of Gemini's intelligence and communication skills.

Power through knowledge, adaptability, and intellect. Magic is learned, recorded, perfected. Often arcanists, researchers, tacticians. Truth has many faces, and they know them all.

## Character Creation

### Hit Dice
1d6 per wizard level

### Primary Ability
Intelligence

### Saving Throw Proficiencies
Intelligence, Wisdom

### Armor Proficiencies
None

### Weapon Proficiencies
Daggers, darts, slings, quarterstaffs, light crossbows

### Skill Choices
**Choose 2 from:** Arcana, History, Insight, Investigation, Medicine, Religion

### Starting Equipment
**Choose (a) or (b) for each:**
- (a) A quarterstaff OR (b) A dagger
- (a) A component pouch OR (b) An arcane focus
- (a) A scholar's pack OR (b) An explorer's pack
- A spellbook and two daggers

### Key Features
- **Spellcasting:** Prepare spells from the largest spell list in the game using Intelligence
- **Spellbook:** Record and learn new spells, can copy spells from scrolls and other spellbooks
- **Arcane Recovery:** Regain some spell slots during short rest (once per day)
- **Ritual Casting:** Cast prepared spells as rituals without using spell slots

### Arcane Traditions (Subclass at Level 2)
**Choose one:**
- **School of Evocation:** Master of damage spells, sculpt spells to protect allies
- **School of Abjuration:** Protective magic, enhanced wards and counterspells
- **School of Divination:** See the future, manipulate fate with Portent
- **School of Conjuration:** Summon creatures and objects, teleportation
- **School of Enchantment:** Charm and manipulate minds
- **School of Illusion:** Create powerful illusions, make them semi-real
- **School of Necromancy:** Command undead, drain life force
- **School of Transmutation:** Transform matter, create transmuter's stone
- **War Magic:** Combine magic and defense, tactical spellcasting
- **Chronurgy Magic:** Manipulate time and fate
- **Graviturgy Magic:** Control gravity and weight

### 💨 Elemental Affinity: Air
**Recommended School:** School of Evocation (Thunder/Lightning focus) - Master air-based damage spells while protecting allies from the storm.

**Alternative Schools:**
- School of Divination - See through the winds of fate
- School of Illusion - Create illusions light as air
- Chronurgy Magic - Time flows like wind

**Elemental Flavor:** Your spells arrive on gusts of wind, your magic crackles with lightning, your presence feels breezy and ethereal.

**Recommended Spells:** Gust, Thunderwave, Levitate, Fly, Lightning Bolt, Wind Wall, Cloudkill, Chain Lightning, Investiture of Wind

### Starting Spells in Spellbook
**6 level-1 spells of your choice from the wizard spell list**

Popular choices: Mage Armor, Shield, Magic Missile, Detect Magic, Identify, Find Familiar

## Traits

**Recommended Ability Scores:** INT (highest), DEX (high), CON (moderate)

**Suggested Spell Packages:**
- **Dual Scholar:** Mix of damage and utility (Magic Missile, Shield, Mage Armor, Detect Magic, Find Familiar, Identify)
- **Versatile Sage:** Control and support (Sleep, Charm Person, Grease, Feather Fall, Comprehend Languages, Alarm)
- **Knowledge Seeker:** Divination and investigation (Detect Magic, Identify, Comprehend Languages, Find Familiar, Alarm, Unseen Servant)

## Strengths

Largest spell selection, incredible versatility, ritual casting, can learn spells from scrolls and other spellbooks, solutions for almost any problem

## Weaknesses

Lowest hit points, no armor proficiency, vulnerable in melee, spell preparation requires planning, expensive to copy spells

## Compatibility

Essential utility caster for any party. Needs protection from frontline fighters. Synergizes with anyone who can keep enemies away
