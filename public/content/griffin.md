# Griffin 🦁

## Class: Sky Knight

**The Noble Guardian**

## Origin

Persian/Greek Mythology

## Element

☀️ Light and Air

## Domain

Honor and Justice

## Description

The Griffin Sky Knight merges the king of beasts with the king of birds, embodying nobility, courage, and divine justice. These warriors are mounted champions who patrol the skies, defending the innocent and punishing the wicked. They combine aerial superiority with righteous fury, wielding blessed armoury and commanding the respect of all who witness their golden wings.

Power through honor, aerial dominance, and divine mandate. They are the embodiment of chivalric virtue, champions who fight not for glory but for justice itself.

## Traits

- Aerial Mastery: Unmatched flight and aerial combat
- Divine Strike: Holy-infused attacks against evil
- Majestic Aura: Inspires allies and demoralizes foes

## Strengths

- Superior aerial mobility
- Effective against evil/undead enemies
- Leadership and inspirational presence

## Weaknesses

- Code of honor limits tactical flexibility
- Vulnerable to anti-air abilities
- Less effective against neutral or good targets

## Compatibility

Natural leaders of righteous causes. Works well with other lawful warriors. Conflicts with morally ambiguous or chaotic allies.
