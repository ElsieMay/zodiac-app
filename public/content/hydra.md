# Hydra 🐍

## Class: Regenerator

**The Many-Headed Terror**

## Origin

Greek Mythology

## Element

💧 Water and Poison

## Domain

Adaptation and Persistence

## Description

The Hydra Regenerator embodies relentless adaptation and nearly immortal endurance. Like the legendary multi-headed serpent, these warriors grow stronger as the battle continues. Each wound spawns new power, each defeat becomes a lesson, and death itself is merely temporary. They command poison, regeneration, and overwhelming multi-target capabilities.

Power through adaptation, persistence, and overwhelming force through numbers. They cannot be defeated through attrition, only through overwhelming them before they adapt to every strategy.

## Traits

- Regenerative Growth: Heal rapidly and grow stronger when damaged
- Multi-Strike: Attack multiple targets simultaneously
- Toxic Blood: Damage enemies who wound you

## Strengths

- Exceptional survivability
- Becomes stronger as battle continues
- Effective against multiple opponents

## Weaknesses

- Weak in initial engagement
- Vulnerable to burst damage before adaptation
- Fire and cauterization prevent regeneration

## Compatibility

Ideal for prolonged engagements. Works well with classes that can survive until Hydra adapts. Struggles in quick skirmishes.
