# Leo ♌

## Class: Paladin

**The Leo Champion**

## Dates

## Element

☀️ Fire

## Ruling Planet

## Description

The Paladin embodies Leo's courage, charisma, and leadership, standing as a shining beacon of hope and honor. Their Oath of Devotion reflects the radiance of Leo, with a focus on heroism, loyalty, and honor. Their powers reflect the sun (Leo's ruling planet), providing inspirational auras, flames, or summoned lion spirits.

Power through conviction, honor, and leadership. Magic manifests as radiant force and inspiring presence. Often champions, holy warriors, or icons of hope. Their belief becomes law on the battlefield.

## Character Creation

### Hit Dice

1d10 per paladin level

### Primary Ability

Strength and Charisma

### Saving Throw Proficiencies

Wisdom, Charisma

### Armor Proficiencies

All armor, shields

### Armoury Proficiencies

Simple armoury, martial armoury

### Skill Choices

**Choose 2 from:** Athletics, Insight, Intimidation, Medicine, Persuasion, Religion

### Starting Equipment

**Choose (a) or (b) for each:**

- (a) A martial Armoury and a shield OR (b) Two martial armoury
- (a) Five javelins OR (b) Any simple melee armoury
- (a) A priest's pack OR (b) An explorer's pack
- Chain mail and a holy symbol

### Key Features

- **Divine Sense:** Detect celestials, fiends, and undead (CHA modifier + 1 per day)
- **Lay on Hands:** Heal HP pool equal to paladin level × 5, restore HP or cure disease
- **Fighting Style:** Choose at level 2 (Defense, Dueling, Great Armoury Fighting, Protection)
- **Spellcasting:** Cast divine spells using Charisma (starting at level 2)
- **Divine Smite:** Expend spell slots to deal extra radiant damage on melee hits
- **Aura of Protection:** You and nearby allies add your CHA modifier to saves (level 6)

### Sacred Oaths (Subclass at Level 3)

**Choose one:**

- **Oath of Devotion:** Classic knight, protection and radiant damage
- **Oath of the Ancients:** Nature guardian, fey magic and preservation
- **Oath of Vengeance:** Relentless hunter of evil, mobility and damage
- **Oath of Conquest:** Dominate enemies through fear and control
- **Oath of Redemption:** Peaceful protector, prevent violence when possible
- **Oath of Glory:** Champion athlete, inspire and lead through deeds
- **Oath of the Watchers:** Vigilant against extraplanar threats
- **Oath of the Crown:** Loyal to civilization and law

### ☀️ Elemental Affinity: Fire

**Recommended Oath:** Oath of Glory - Channel the sun's radiant power and inspirational flames befitting Leo's solar nature.

**Alternative Oaths:**

- Oath of Devotion - Radiant, sun-blessed paladin
- Oath of the Crown - Royal flames of leadership

**Elemental Flavor:** Your Divine Smite manifests as solar fire, your Lay on Hands glows with warm light, your aura radiates like the sun itself.

**Recommended Spells:** Searing Smite, Branding Smite, Daylight, Flame Strike

### Fighting Styles

- **Defense:** +1 AC while wearing armor
- **Dueling:** +2 damage when wielding one armoury and no other armoury
- **Great Armoury Fighting:** Reroll 1s and 2s on damage dice with two-handed armoury
- **Protection:** Impose disadvantage on attacks against nearby allies

## Traits

**Recommended Ability Scores:** STR (highest), CHA (high), CON (moderate)

**Suggested Equipment Packages:**

- **Solar Champion:** Longsword + shield, javelins, Defense fighting style
- **Lion's Pride:** Greatsword, two-handed, Great Armoury Fighting style
- **Radiant Leader:** Warhammer + shield, priest's pack, Persuasion and Religion skills

## Strengths

Exceptional durability, strong melee damage with Divine Smite, healing capability, aura buffs allies, high saves, immune to disease

## Weaknesses

Multiple ability score dependencies (STR/CHA), limited spell slots, oaths restrict behavior, weaker at range

## Compatibility

Natural party leader and tank. Works excellently with all classes. Aura of Protection benefits entire party. Pairs well with spellcasters who stay near
