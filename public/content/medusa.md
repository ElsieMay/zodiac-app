# Medusa 👁️

## Class: Petrifier

**The Cursed Gorgon**

## Origin

Greek Mythology

## Element

🗿 Stone and Curse

## Domain

Transformation and Isolation

## Description

The Medusa Petrifier wields one of mythology's most feared powers—the ability to turn living beings to stone with a mere glance. Once a beautiful maiden cursed by the gods, Medusa represents tragic power and isolation. These warriors master petrification magic, serpentine summons, and curse manipulation. They are tactical controllers who reshape the battlefield by transforming enemies into obstacles.

Power through fear, transformation, and tactical control. Enemies must fight without looking, making every engagement psychological warfare. They are masters of the battlefield, turning foes into statues and allies into believers.

## Traits

- Petrifying Gaze: Turn enemies to stone temporarily or permanently
- Serpent Hair: Autonomous serpent attacks
- Curse Weaving: Debilitate enemies with powerful hexes

## Strengths

- Ultimate crowd control ability
- Strong defensive capabilities
- Psychological advantage in combat

## Weaknesses

- Requires line of sight for gaze
- Mirrors and reflections can backfire
- Socially isolated due to power's danger

## Compatibility

Essential for controlling battlefield flow. Works well with mobile allies who can capitalize on petrified foes. Dangerous to undisciplined allies.
