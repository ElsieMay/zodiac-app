# Nemean Lion 🦁

## Class: Invulnerable Warrior

**The Unbreakable Beast**

## Origin

Greek Mythology

## Element

☀️ Solar Might

## Domain

Invincibility and Pride

## Description

The Nemean Lion Warrior embodies absolute physical supremacy and legendary invulnerability. Named for the beast whose hide could not be pierced by any mortal weapon, these warriors possess nearly impenetrable defenses. Their golden fur deflects blades, their roar shatters morale, and their strength tears through armor as if it were parchment. They are living fortresses, walking symbols of unconquerable will.

Power through invincibility, raw strength, and overwhelming presence. Normal weapons cannot harm them, forcing enemies to seek alternative strategies or face inevitable defeat.

## Traits

- Impenetrable Hide: Immunity or high resistance to physical damage
- Solar Roar: Devastating sonic attack that breaks formations
- Predator's Might: Enhanced strength and unarmed combat

## Strengths

- Exceptional physical durability
- Overwhelming melee power
- Psychological impact on enemies

## Weaknesses

- Vulnerable to magic and elemental attacks
- Limited ranged options
- Overconfidence due to invulnerability

## Compatibility

Frontline tank essential for any dangerous mission. Works well with magic users who can handle threats beyond physical reach. Clashes with other pride-driven warriors.
