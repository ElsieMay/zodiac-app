# Pegasus 🐴

## Class: Sky Dancer

**The Winged Divine**

## Origin

Greek Mythology

## Element

☁️ Air and Light

## Domain

Freedom and Inspiration

## Description

The Pegasus Sky Dancer channels the grace and majesty of the divine winged horse. Born from Medusa's blood, Pegasus represents freedom, inspiration, and the transcendence of earthly limitations. These warriors are unmatched in aerial mobility, wielding light-based magic and inspirational auras. They move like lightning across the battlefield, striking with precision before vanishing into the clouds.

Power through speed, grace, and divine inspiration. They are poetry in motion, warriors who fight not with brutality but with elegance, inspiring allies while frustrating enemies who can never catch them.

## Traits

- Divine Flight: Unmatched aerial mobility and speed
- Inspiring Presence: Buffs and motivates nearby allies
- Lightning Strike: Swift, precise attacks from above

## Strengths

- Superior mobility and evasion
- Strong support and inspiration abilities
- Effective hit-and-run tactics

## Weaknesses

- Fragile when grounded or caught
- Limited sustained damage output
- Requires open spaces to maximize potential

## Compatibility

Ideal for mobile warfare and supporting ground forces. Works well with coordinated teams. Struggles when forced into static combat.
