# Phoenix 🔥

## Class: Eternal Flame

**The Immortal Reborn**

## Origin

Egyptian/Greek Mythology

## Element

🔥 Sacred Fire

## Domain

Rebirth and Immortality

## Description

The Phoenix Eternal Flame is the ultimate survivor, embodying the cycle of death and rebirth. These warriors cannot truly die—when struck down, they burst into flames and are reborn from their own ashes, often stronger than before. They command sacred fire that both destroys and heals, purging corruption while cauterizing wounds. Their presence alone brings hope to allies and despair to enemies who realize death is not an option.

Power through immortality, transformation, and eternal flames. They are living symbols of resilience and renewal, warriors who become stronger with each defeat, embodying the truth that what doesn't kill them literally makes them stronger.

## Traits

- Rebirth: Resurrect from death with restored power
- Healing Flames: Fire that damages enemies but heals allies
- Eternal Endurance: Enhanced stamina and resistance to exhaustion

## Strengths

- Effective immortality through rebirth
- Versatile fire magic (offense and healing)
- Demoralizing to enemies

## Weaknesses

- Rebirth has cooldown or resource cost
- Vulnerable during resurrection process
- Ice and water magic can prevent rebirth

## Compatibility

Essential for high-risk missions where death is likely. Works well with any team composition. Becomes focal point of enemy attention.
