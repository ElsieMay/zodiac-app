# Sagittarius ♐

## Class: Ranger

**The Sagittarius Seeker**

## Dates


## Element

🏹 Fire

## Ruling Planet


## Description

The Ranger channels Sagittarius' adventurous spirit, focusing on exploration, freedom, and long-range combat. Their hunter's mark could be flavored as an astrological sign, tracking enemies based on their Zodiac traits or celestial position. Beast Master companions are spiritual animals drawn from Sagittarius' connection to the hunt, or even celestial beasts.

Power through freedom, exploration, and precision. Magic enhances movement, tracking, and the hunt. Often scouts, explorers, monster hunters. No horizon is ever far enough.

## Character Creation

### Hit Dice
1d10 per ranger level

### Primary Ability
Dexterity and Wisdom

### Saving Throw Proficiencies
Strength, Dexterity

### Armor Proficiencies
Light armor, medium armor, shields

### Weapon Proficiencies
Simple weapons, martial weapons

### Skill Choices
**Choose 3 from:** Animal Handling, Athletics, Insight, Investigation, Nature, Perception, Stealth, Survival

### Starting Equipment
**Choose (a) or (b) for each:**
- (a) Scale mail OR (b) Leather armor
- (a) Two shortswords OR (b) Two simple melee weapons
- (a) A dungeoneer's pack OR (b) An explorer's pack
- A longbow and a quiver of 20 arrows

### Key Features
- **Favored Enemy:** Choose enemy type(s), gain advantages against them
- **Natural Explorer:** Choose terrain type(s), gain bonuses in that terrain
- **Fighting Style:** Choose at level 2 (Archery, Defense, Dueling, Two-Weapon Fighting)
- **Spellcasting:** Cast nature spells using Wisdom (starting at level 2)
- **Primeval Awareness:** Detect certain creature types
- **Extra Attack:** Attack twice per action (level 5)
- **Land's Stride:** Move through difficult terrain easily
- **Hide in Plain Sight:** Enhanced camouflage (level 10)

### Ranger Archetypes (Subclass at Level 3)
**Choose one:**
- **Hunter:** Classic versatile ranger, multiple combat options
- **Beast Master:** Animal companion that fights alongside you
- **Gloom Stalker:** Darkvision, invisible to darkvision, ambush attacks
- **Horizon Walker:** Planar warrior, teleportation, detect portals
- **Monster Slayer:** Specialize in hunting specific creatures, protective magic
- **Fey Wanderer:** Charm and confusion, fey summons, wisdom for social checks
- **Swarmkeeper:** Swarm of nature spirits aid in attacks and movement
- **Drakewarden:** Drake companion that grows with you

### 🔥 Elemental Affinity: Fire
**Recommended Archetype:** Drakewarden (Fire Drake) - Bond with a fire drake companion, shoot flame-infused arrows.

**Alternative Archetypes:**
- Hunter - Versatile fire-wielding adventurer
- Fey Wanderer - Wild, passionate flames of the feywild

**Elemental Flavor:** Your arrows trail fire, your drake breathes flames, your tracking is like following a wildfire's path.

**Recommended Spells:** Entangle (describe as burning vines), Cure Wounds (cauterizing flames), Conjure Animals (fire-aligned beasts)

### Fighting Styles
- **Archery:** +2 bonus to ranged attack rolls
- **Defense:** +1 AC while wearing armor
- **Dueling:** +2 damage when wielding one weapon and no other weapons
- **Two-Weapon Fighting:** Add ability modifier to damage of off-hand attacks

### Favored Enemy Options
Choose from: Aberrations, Beasts, Celestials, Constructs, Dragons, Elementals, Fey, Fiends, Giants, Monstrosities, Oozes, Plants, Undead, or two humanoid races

### Natural Explorer Terrains
Choose from: Arctic, Coast, Desert, Forest, Grassland, Mountain, Swamp, Underdark

## Traits

**Recommended Ability Scores:** DEX (highest), WIS (high), CON (moderate)

**Suggested Equipment Packages:**
- **Archer Seeker:** Longbow, leather armor, Archery fighting style
- **Dual-Wielding Explorer:** Two shortswords, medium armor, Two-Weapon Fighting
- **Versatile Traveler:** Longsword + shield, scale mail, Defense or Dueling

**Suggested Skill Selections:**
- Wilderness Scout: Stealth, Perception, Survival
- Monster Hunter: Investigation, Nature, Perception
- Party Face: Insight, Animal Handling, Perception

## Strengths

Excellent at exploration and tracking, strong ranged combat, versatile with spells and martial prowess, extra attack, good skill selection

## Weaknesses

Divided focus between martial and spellcasting, favored enemy/terrain can be too specific, fewer spell slots than full casters, lacks heavy armor

## Compatibility

Works well in wilderness and exploration campaigns. Excellent scout and damage dealer. Pairs well with melee fighters and spellcasters who handle social situations
