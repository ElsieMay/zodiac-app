# Taurus ♉

## Class: Warlock

**The Taurus Pacts**

## Dates

## Element

🌿 Earth

## Ruling Planet

## Description

The Warlock channels Taurus' grounded, loyal, and material-driven nature, forming pacts with powerful entities to gain long-lasting, stable power. The Fiend or Archfey pact reflects Taurus' need for security and stability, drawing upon ancient, reliable forces. Their abilities are flavored around the earth, fertility, and wealth, with powers that enhance resilience, physicality, and prosperity.

Power through ancient pacts and enduring bonds. Magic is stable, reliable, but comes at a cost. Patrons may be star-beasts, elder constellations. Loyalty is power—and power remembers.

## Character Creation

### Hit Dice

1d8 per warlock level

### Primary Ability

Charisma

### Saving Throw Proficiencies

Wisdom, Charisma

### Armor Proficiencies

Light armor

### Armoury Proficiencies

Simple armoury

### Skill Choices

**Choose 2 from:** Arcana, Deception, History, Intimidation, Investigation, Nature, Religion

### Starting Equipment

**Choose (a) or (b) for each:**

- (a) A light crossbow and 20 bolts OR (b) Any simple armoury
- (a) A component pouch OR (b) An arcane focus
- (a) A scholar's pack OR (b) A dungeoneer's pack
- Leather armor, any simple armoury, and two daggers

### Key Features

- **Otherworldly Patron:** Make a pact with a powerful entity (chosen at level 1)
- **Pact Magic:** Cast spells using Charisma, regain spell slots on short rest
- **Eldritch Invocations:** Customize with magical abilities (starting at level 2)
- **Pact Boon:** Receive a special gift from patron at level 3 (Pact of the Chain, Blade, or Tome)

### Otherworldly Patrons (Subclass at Level 1)

**Choose one:**

- **The Archfey:** Fey magic, charm and teleportation abilities
- **The Fiend:** Dark magic, temporary HP on kills, resistance to fire
- **The Great Old One:** Telepathy, mind manipulation, psychic damage
- **The Celestial:** Healing magic, radiant damage, sacred flame
- **The Hexblade:** Combat prowess, curse enemies, armoury summoning
- **The Fathomless:** Tentacle attacks, water breathing, cold damage
- **The Genie:** Elemental damage, genie's vessel for rest

### 🪨 Elemental Affinity: Earth

**Recommended Patron:** The Genie (Dao - Earth Genie) - Bond with an earth elemental being, gain earth-based powers and a protective vessel.

**Alternative Patrons:**

- The Archfey - Earthly, natural fey magic
- The Fiend - Grounded, material power

**Elemental Flavor:** Your Eldritch Blast appears as stone shards, your pact manifests through gems and crystals, your magic feels solid and tangible.

**Recommended Invocations:**

- Lance of Lethargy (slowing like mud)
- Armor of Shadows (stone skin)
- Gift of the Depths (earth includes water)

**Recommended Spells:** Armor of Agathys (stone armor), Earthbind, Maximilian's Earthen Grasp, Stone Shape

### Eldritch Invocations (Examples)

- **Agonizing Blast:** Add CHA modifier to eldritch blast damage
- **Devil's Sight:** See in magical darkness
- **Mask of Many Faces:** Cast disguise self at will
- **Misty Visions:** Cast silent image at will

## Traits

**Recommended Ability Scores:** CHA (highest), CON (high), DEX (moderate)

**Suggested Spell Packages:**

- **Earthbound Pact:** Armor of Agathys, Hex, Hold Person
- **Sensual Deceiver:** Charm Person, Disguise Self, Suggestion
- **Material Guardian:** Shield, Comprehend Languages, Detect Magic

## Strengths

Versatile spellcasting, short rest recovery, powerful Eldritch Blast cantrip, highly customizable through invocations

## Weaknesses

Limited spell slots (but regain on short rest), fewer spells known than wizards, light armor only, pact obligations

## Compatibility

Flexible role - can deal damage, control, or support. Works well in any party composition. Pairs with martial classes for battlefield control
