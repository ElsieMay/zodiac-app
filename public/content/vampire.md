# Vampire 🦇

## Class: Blood Lord

**The Eternal Nightstalker**

## Origin

European Folklore

## Element

🩸 Blood and Shadow

## Domain

Night and Immortality

## Description

The Vampire Blood Lord is a creature of eternal night, wielding the dark powers of blood magic and shadow manipulation. These immortal predators combine supernatural speed, hypnotic charm, and life-draining abilities. They grow stronger by consuming the life force of enemies, transforming into mist or bats, and commanding lesser undead. Masters of both social manipulation and brutal combat, they are as dangerous in court as on the battlefield.

Power through blood, charm, and eternal life. They are aristocrats of darkness, sophisticated predators who see mortals as prey and blood as currency. Immortality grants them patience—they can wait centuries for the perfect moment to strike.

## Traits

- Life Drain: Steal health and power from enemies
- Shadow Form: Transform into mist, bats, or shadow
- Hypnotic Gaze: Charm and control weak-willed victims

## Strengths

- Effective immortality and regeneration
- Versatile combat and social abilities
- Grows stronger by defeating enemies

## Weaknesses

- Vulnerable to sunlight and holy symbols
- Requires regular feeding
- Weaknesses can be exploited (stakes, garlic, running water)

## Compatibility

Excels in nighttime operations and social infiltration. Works well with other night creatures. Conflicts with holy warriors and those who value life.
